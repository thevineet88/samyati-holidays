Day by Day

Detailed Itinerary

Reporting Station: Mumbai Central Railway Station. Reporting Time: 8:00 PM, 15 January 2026. Train: Saurashtra Mail. Board train for Dwarka. Overnight journey.

🍲 No Meals

Reach Dwarka Railway Station by approximately 1:15 PM. Proceed to hotel and check in. Lunch at own cost. Visit Dwarkadhish Temple and Rukmini Temple. Return to hotel, dinner and rest. Overnight at Dwarka.

🍲 Dinner

Breakfast and hotel checkout. Visit Bet Dwarka and Nageshwar Temple. Lunch en route at own expense. Continue to Somnath (4.5 hrs). Evening visit to Somnath Jyotirlinga. Check in at hotel, dinner and rest.

🍲 Breakfast, Dinner

Breakfast and checkout. Visit Bhalka Teerth, Geeta Mandir, and Triveni Sangam. Travel towards Girnar (2.5 hrs). Reach Girnar, check in at hotel. Dinner and rest. Overnight at Girnar/Junagadh.

🍲 Breakfast, Dinner

Start early for Girnar Darshan. Breakfast en route at own expense. Reach Girnar Ropeway point and take ropeway to top. Complete Girnar Darshan. Ropeway tickets must be booked online at udankhatola.com (time slot: 7:00 AM - 8:00 AM). Return to hotel. Dinner and rest.

🍲 Dinner

Breakfast. Check out and proceed towards Junagadh Railway Station. Board return train (Saurashtra Janta Express, 19218) at approximately 12:58 PM. Overnight journey to Mumbai.

🍲 Breakfast

Reach Mumbai at approximately 6:00 AM. Return home with sweet memories!

🍲 No Meals
