Important Advisory

Shimla and Manali are hill stations with cool temperatures. Carry warm clothing, comfortable walking shoes, and sunscreen. Atal Tunnel and snow points require warm gear. Carry valid photo ID proof. Not recommended for infants below 3 years or senior citizens with health issues.

  • • Carry warm clothing. Snow activities at own cost.
  • • Smoking and drinking is strictly prohibited during travel and at sightseeing places.
  • • Sightseeing schedule is subject to change as per on-day circumstances.
  • • Good physical fitness recommended for trekking activities.
  • • Tour operators have the right to make on-spot changes for unforeseen events.
