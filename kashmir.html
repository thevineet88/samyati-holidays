Day by Day

Detailed Itinerary

Board Train from Mumbaiyour train journey. Here begins our journey to the amazing North. Have a deep sleep to get refreshed for next day and start the fun-trip. Reporting Station: Mumbai Central Railway Station. Board train for Amritsar. Overnight journey. 06 February 2026 (Friday)

Enjoy the train journey with scenic views of the countryside. Reach Amritsar by morning. Check in at hotel. Freshen up. Evening visit to Golden Temple. Dinner and overnight stay at Amritsar. 07 February 2026 (Saturday)

After breakfast, visit Golden Temple and Jallianwala Bagh. After lunch, visit Attari-Wagha Border for the flag ceremony. Evening board bus for Pahalgam. Overnight journey. 08 February 2026 (Sunday)

Reach Pahalgam by 08:00 AM. Check in at hotel. Freshen up. After breakfast, head towards Betaab Valley, a famous Bollywood shooting location. Enjoy the scenic beauty of the valley. Dinner and overnight stay. 09 February 2026 (Monday)

After breakfast, check out and head towards Srinagar (3 hrs). Check in at houseboat on Dal Lake. After lunch, proceed for Srinagar sightseeing. Visit Mughal Gardens and local markets. Dinner and overnight stay in houseboat. 10 February 2026 (Tuesday)

OVERNIGHT STAY IN SRINAGAR11 FEBRUARY 2026(Wednesday) Full day excursion to ‘The Meadows of Gold’, also the last stop of Kashmir. the sledding ride & horse ride (at your own cost). to Hotel.

OVERNIGHT STAY IN SRINAGAR12 FEBRUARY 2026(Thursday) Catch some rays at ‘The Meadows of Flowers’. Get ready to witness breathtaking views from Gondola ride. Gondola ride is the second highest cable ride in the world. This ride comprises of 2 stages : Phase 1 (Gulmarg to Kongdoori Peak) &

Early morning (04:00 AM) check out from hotel. Start journey to Amritsar (10 hrs). Enroute breakfast at local dhaba. Reach Amritsar by afternoon. Board train for Mumbai. Overnight journey. 13 February 2026 (Friday)

Return train journey to Mumbai. Reach Mumbai by approximately 11:55 PM on 14 February 2026. Return home with sweet memories!
