How It All Started

From a Group of Friends to 20,000+ Happy Travellers

Every great journey begins with a single step. Ours began with a simple idea.

We are a group of engineering graduates who always dreamed of building something of our own. Like many students, we had the option of following a conventional career path. But deep down, we knew we wanted to create experiences, not just earn a living.

The travel industry was completely new to us. We had no business background, no years of experience, and no roadmap to follow. What we did have was a passion for exploring, the confidence to connect with people, and the belief that travel has the power to bring strangers together and turn them into lifelong friends.

Before Samyati Holidays officially existed, we organized a trip to Hampi for a small group of friends, simply for the joy of travelling together. We never imagined that this one trip would change everything.

During the journey, something extraordinary happened. People who had never met before started sharing stories, laughing together, helping one another, and creating memories that would stay with them forever. By the end of the trip, they weren't just fellow travellers, they had become a family.

That experience taught us an important lesson: people don't just travel to visit new places; they travel to discover new connections, unforgettable moments, and a different version of themselves.

That single trip gave us the confidence to dream bigger.

And that's how Samyati Holidays was born.
