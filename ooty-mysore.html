Day by Day

Detailed Itinerary

Reporting Station : CSMT : 07:00 AM 16 April 2026 (Thursday) Mumbai to Bangalore till 06:00 AM

After breakfast, proceed for Mysore sightseeing. Visit Mysore Zoo, Mysore Palace, and Krishnaraja Sagar (KRS) Dam. Experience the royal heritage of the city. Dinner and overnight stay. 17 April 2026 (Friday)

After breakfast, check out from hotel. Departure to Ooty (3-4 hrs). Check in at hotel. After rest, visit Doddabetta Park and Botanical Garden. Enjoy the cool Nilgiri air. Dinner and overnight stay. 18 April 2026 (Saturday)

Breakfast Dinner & rest 19 April 2026 (Sunday) 1.Dolphin’s Nose 2.Sim’s Park

After breakfast, check out from hotel. Proceed to Coimbatore (3 hrs). Visit the magnificent Adiyogi Shiva statue, the 112-ft iconic landmark. Spend time in meditation and reflection. Dinner and overnight stay. 20 April 2026 (Monday)

After breakfast, check out from hotel. Board train for Mumbai. Reach Mumbai (Panvel station) by 05:00 PM. Return home with sweet memories! 21 April 2026 (Tuesday)
