Day by Day

Detailed Itinerary

Reporting Station: Mumbai Central Railway Station (MMCT). Train departure: 10:00 AM. Board train for Amritsar/Himachal Pradesh. Overnight journey.

🍲 No Meals

Continue train journey. Reach Amritsar Junction by approximately 8:15 PM. Proceed to hotel, check in, take rest. Overnight at hotel in Amritsar.

🍲 Dinner

Breakfast at hotel. Visit Attari-Wagah Border. Visit Golden Temple in the evening. Free time for shopping at local market. Return to hotel, dinner, leisure time. Overnight at Amritsar.

🍲 Breakfast, Dinner

Early breakfast and check out. Journey towards Dharamshala. Visit HPCA Cricket Stadium, BhagsuNag Waterfall, Dalai Lama Temple, and St. John Church. Check in at hotel, dinner, rest. Overnight at Dharamshala.

🍲 Breakfast, Dinner

Early breakfast and check out. Journey towards Manali. Visit Hidimba Temple, Ghatotkach Temple, Tibetan Monastery, and Mall Road. Check in at hotel, dinner, rest. Overnight at Manali.

🍲 Breakfast, Dinner

Breakfast. Proceed to Solang Valley for snow activities. Return to hotel, enjoy DJ Night, dinner, rest. Snow activities and clothes at own cost.

🍲 Breakfast, Dinner

Breakfast and check out. Journey towards Manikaran. Visit Hot Water Springs and Manikaran Gurudwara. Continue to Kasol campsite. Leisure time, dinner, tent stay. Overnight at Kasol.

🍲 Breakfast, Dinner

Breakfast and check out. Proceed towards Ambala Cantt Junction. Opportunity for river rafting and paragliding (at own cost). Catch return train in the evening. Overnight train journey.

🍲 Breakfast

Return train journey to Mumbai. Expected arrival approximately 11:55 PM. Return home with sweet memories!

🍲 No Meals
