Day by Day

Detailed Itinerary

26 March 2026 (Thursday) — Reporting Station: Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT). Board the overnight train for Kodaikanal. Train departure: 8:00 PM. Overnight journey.

🍲 No Meals

27 March 2026 (Friday) — Enjoy the scenic train journey. Watch the landscapes change as you travel south. Overnight on train.

🍲 No Meals

28 March 2026 (Saturday) — Reach Kodaikanal Road station by 4:00 AM. Freshen up at the station. Transfer to hotel and check in (early check-in subject to availability). After breakfast, begin local sightseeing. Enroute lunch (at own cost).

🍲 Breakfast & Dinner

29 March 2026 (Sunday) — After breakfast, proceed for full-day Kodaikanal sightseeing. Visit Bryant Park, Coaker's Walk, Pillar Rocks, and other scenic points. Enroute lunch (at own cost). Return to hotel.

🍲 Breakfast & Dinner

30 March 2026 (Monday) — After breakfast, continue sightseeing. Visit Dolphin Nose, Pine Forest, and other attractions. Enroute lunch (at own cost). Return to hotel for dinner.

🍲 Breakfast & Dinner

31 March 2026 (Tuesday) — After breakfast, check out from hotel. Begin return journey to Mumbai with memories of the trip with your travel buddies.

🍲 Breakfast & Dinner

01 April 2026 (Wednesday) — Reach Mumbai by approximately 6:00 AM. Return home with sweet memories!

🍲 No Meals
