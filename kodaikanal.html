Day by Day

Detailed Itinerary

Reporting Station: Chhtrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT). Reporting Time: 08:00 PM. Board train to Kodaikanal. Overnight journey. (Pune Junction pickup at 11:30 PM for Pune joiners). 26 March 2026 (Thursday)

🍲 Breakfast & Dinner

Enjoy the train journey through the pleasant and scenic beauty of mother nature enroute. Overnight train journey. 27 March 2026 (Friday)

🍲 Breakfast & Dinner

Reach Kodaikanal Road Station early morning (approx. 04:00 AM). Freshen up at the station waiting room. After breakfast, proceed to Kodaikanal by bus (3 hrs). Check in at hotel. Dinner and overnight stay. 28 March 2026 (Saturday)

🍲 Breakfast & Dinner

After breakfast, proceed for Kodaikanal sightseeing. Visit Bryant Park, Coaker's Walk, Pillar Rocks, and Green Valley View. Enroute lunch (at own cost). Return to hotel. Dinner and overnight stay. 29 March 2026 (Sunday)

🍲 Breakfast & Dinner

After breakfast, check out and head for more Kodaikanal sightseeing. Visit Vattakanal View Point, Pine Forest, Moir's Point, and Dolphin Nose. Enroute lunch (at own cost). Return to hotel. Dinner and overnight stay. 30 March 2026 (Monday)

🍲 Breakfast & Dinner

Enjoy breakfast and check out from hotel. Board return train for Mumbai. Enjoy the return journey, memorizing the fun moments spent with fellow travellers. 31 March 2026 (Tuesday)

🍲 Breakfast & Dinner

Reach Pune Junction by 02:00 AM. Reach Mumbai (Dadar) by 06:00 AM on 01 April 2026. Return home with sweet memories!

Important Advisory

Carry warm clothing as Kodaikanal is a hill station with cool temperatures. Wear comfortable walking shoes for sightseeing. Carry valid photo ID proof. Some sightseeing spots involve steep paths. Not recommended for infants below 3 years or senior citizens with health issues.
